Frequently Asked Questions

Authoritative answers regarding amenities, location, and your stay at Diamante Del Mar.

Is the tap water safe to drink (potable)?

Yes, the tap water within all suites at Diamante Del Mar is potable and completely safe for consumption. We encourage guests to drink tap water to reduce plastic waste during their stay on Kos island.

Why isn't the air conditioning working?

Every renovated suite features independent climate control. For optimal performance, all doors, balcony doors, and windows must be tightly closed. A sensor automatically deactivates the system if an open door is detected to maintain the ideal climate.

What is the Wi-Fi speed and password?

We offer free, high-speed Wi-Fi (+50MB) throughout all areas. This is ideal for streaming content on our Smart TVs or for remote work if needed. The password is provided upon arrival.

How close is the property to Lambi Beach and Kos Town center?

Phaedra’s location is ideal. Diamante Del Mar is just 200 meters—approximately a 3-minute walk—from the renowned Lambi Beach and a short 1.1km walk from Kos Harbor and the historic center.

How does check-in work and what are the times?

Check-in is after 16:00, and check-out is by 11:00 AM. We utilize a keyless entry system (smart lock) with a unique code, allowing for flexible self-check-in at any time, even late at night, without requiring an in-person meeting.

Where is the nearest supermarket?

The highly accessible 'AB Vassilopoulos' supermarket is located just a 3-minute (250m) walk from the suites.

Can I store my luggage after check-out?

Yes, if your departure is after 11:00 AM, we provide secure luggage storage space on the third floor of the building, which is monitored by security cameras for your absolute peace of mind.
