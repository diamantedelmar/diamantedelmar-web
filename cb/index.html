Host Secrets

Beyond the kitchen, here are our personal recommendations to make your stay at Diamante del mar unforgettable.

The Hidden Taverna

Just a 10-minute walk down the coastal path, 'Ouzeri Tou Giorgou' serves the best fresh grilled octopus in the area. Tell them you're staying with us!

Local Wine Pairing

When cooking the Seafood recipes, we highly recommend picking up a bottle of Assyrtiko from the local market. Its crisp, volcanic minerality is perfect.

Sourcing Ingredients

The Thursday morning farmer's market in the main square is the best place to find ripe tomatoes, fresh feta, and local honey for your Baklava.
