Welcome to Diamante del mar Rhodes

Enhance your stay in Rhodes with curated, tailor-made experiences.

We are delighted to host you! To make your island getaway truly unforgettable, we have partnered with Vibes and View to offer you exclusive, hand-crafted local experiences.

Skip the crowded tourist buses and explore Rhodes in complete comfort with a private driver and a luxury SUV . Whether you want to conquer the island's highest peak or find hidden, untamed beaches, these small-group (2–6 guests) excursions are fully customizable to match your personal style .

The Peak Experience - Morning Edition (Hiking)

Discover the island from above as Rhodes wakes up.

  • A guided hike to the highest mountain of Rhodes, combining nature, light adventure, and breathtaking panoramic views of the Aegean Sea .
  • Scenic drive through the island in a luxury SUV and a relaxed-pace guided hike to the summit .
  • Stop at the traditional village of Zia and visit a peaceful pine forest where peacocks roam freely .
  • What's Included: Luxury SUV transport, a freshly prepared platter of local snacks and seasonal fruits served at the top, fresh seasonal juice in Zia, and bottled water .
⏱ Duration: 6-7 hours 👥 Capacity: 2-6 people 📅 Monday to Saturday

The Peak Experience - Sunset Edition (Hiking)

Golden hour at the highest point of Rhodes.

  • A guided evening hike offering a unique combination of nature, soft adventure, and unforgettable sunset views .
  • Fully private sunset views halfway up the mountain .
  • Ideal for couples and guests seeking a calm, scenic transition from day to dusk .
  • What's Included: Luxury SUV transport, hike to the summit, bottled water, and a choice of a curated platter of local delicacies OR dinner at a traditional restaurant in Zia (dinner at an additional cost) .
⏱ Duration: 5-7 hours 👥 Capacity: 2-6 people 📅 Monday to Saturday

Salty Trails - Beach Hopping & Sunset Dinner

Sun, heritage, and the slow rhythm of the wild west coast.

  • Scenic coastal drive through the island's wild western landscapes .
  • Visit the historic 5th-century basilica of Agios Stefanos .
  • Stops at two secluded golden-sand beaches with crystal-clear waters to relax and swim .
  • What's Included: Luxury SUV transport, historical and beach stops, and a beautiful sunset dinner by the sea . (Water sports and sunbeds are optional and at your own expense ).
⏱ Duration: 7-8 hours 👥 Capacity: 2-6 people 📅 Monday to Saturday

Create Your Rhodes

  • Enjoy the island in complete comfort and exclusivity with your own private driver and luxury SUV for 8 hours .
