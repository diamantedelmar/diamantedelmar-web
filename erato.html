About the Space

Find your zen at Diamante Del Mar Erato. This elegant, newly built apartment creates a warm and harmonious atmosphere through its use of natural stone, floral patterns, and vibrant indoor plants. It perfectly accommodates up to 3 guests with a comfortable Queen-size bed and an additional sofa bed.

The absolute highlight of Erato is the private balcony. Featuring a beautiful hanging swing and a lush green corner with potted plants, it is the ultimate spot to unwind after a day of swimming at Lambi Beach or exploring the archaeological sites of Kos Town.
