Frequently Asked Questions

Authoritative answers to common guest inquiries about your stay at Diamante Del Mar in Kos.

Is the tap water safe to drink?

Yes, the tap water within all suites at Diamante Del Mar is potable and completely safe for consumption. We encourage guests to drink tap water to reduce plastic waste during their stay on Kos island.

Why isn't the air conditioning working?

Every suite, including the recently renovated Phaedra suite, features independent, autonomous air conditioning (A/C). To operate correctly, all doors, balcony doors, and windows must be tightly closed. A sensor features that automatically deactivates the system if an open door is detected to maintain the ideal climate.

What is the Wi-Fi speed and password?

We offer free, high-speed Wi-Fi (+50MB) throughout the property. This is ideal for streaming content on our two Smart TVs or for remote work if needed. The password will be provided upon arrival.

How close is the property to the beach and Kos Town?

Diamante Del Mar is perfectly positioned. We are a 15-minute walk (1.1km) from Kos Town Harbor and just 200 meters—approximately a 3-minute walk—from the renowned Lambi Beach.

How does check-in work and what are the times?

Check-in is after 16:00, and check-out is by 11:00 AM. We utilize a keyless entry system (smart lock) with a unique access code, allowing for flexible self-check-in at any time, even late at night, without requiring an in-person meeting.

Where is the nearest supermarket?

The highly accessible 'AB Vassilopoulos' supermarket is located just a 3-minute (250m) walk from the suites, providing easy access to all your essentials.

Can I store my luggage after check-out?

Yes, if your departure is after 11:00 AM, we provide secure luggage storage space on the third floor of the building, which is monitored by security cameras for your peace of mind.
